# Savings Accounts

Our savings accounts help you grow your money safely.

- Interest is calculated daily on the closing balance and paid on the first business day of each month.
- The Everyday Saver has no minimum balance and allows unlimited withdrawals.
- The Bonus Saver pays a higher rate if you deposit at least $200 each month and make no withdrawals.
- Interest rates are variable and can change. The current rate is always shown on the product page in the mobile app and on our website.
- There are no monthly account-keeping fees on personal savings accounts.

To see your personal rate or balance, sign in to the mobile app or online banking. Support staff cannot read your balance to you over chat for security reasons.
