# Loans & Credit (General Education Only)

This assistant provides general education, not financial or legal advice, and cannot approve credit.

## Personal loans
- Fixed-rate personal loans have set monthly repayments over a chosen term.
- Your rate depends on the amount, term, and a credit assessment.
- Use the loan calculator in the app to estimate repayments before applying.

## Credit cards
- Interest is charged on balances not paid in full by the due date.
- Paying the full statement balance each month avoids interest on purchases.
- Cash advances usually attract interest immediately with no interest-free period.

## Applications, approvals & limits
- All applications and credit-limit changes go through a secure, identity-verified flow and a credit decision.
- Support chat cannot approve, decline, or change a credit limit; it can only explain the process.

For advice specific to your situation, consider speaking to a licensed financial adviser.
