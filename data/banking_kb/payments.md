# Payments, Transfers & Standing Orders (Informational Only)

This assistant is non-transactional: it explains how payments work but never moves money.

## Domestic transfers
- Instant payments between local banks usually arrive within seconds, up to a daily limit shown in the app.
- Standard transfers may take up to one business day.

## International / wire transfers
- International transfers require the recipient's IBAN/SWIFT and can take 1–5 business days.
- Fees and exchange rates are shown before you confirm in the app.

## Standing orders & direct debits
- A standing order is a fixed recurring payment you control.
- A direct debit lets a merchant collect variable amounts you authorised.
- You can view, pause, or cancel these in the app under Payments > Scheduled.

To actually create, approve, or cancel any payment you must use the secure mobile app or online banking, which authenticate you and record your consent. Chat support cannot execute or approve payments.
