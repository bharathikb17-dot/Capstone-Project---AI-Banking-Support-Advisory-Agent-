# Fraud, Disputes & Security

If you suspect fraud or an unauthorised transaction, act quickly.

## Immediate steps
1. Freeze the affected card in the app (Card Controls > Freeze).
2. Report it via "Report & replace" or the 24/7 fraud line on our website.
3. Do not share your PIN, full card number, one-time codes, or passwords with anyone — including anyone claiming to be from the bank.

## Disputes
- You can raise a transaction dispute in the app under Transactions > Report a problem.
- Provisional credit may be issued while an investigation is underway, subject to policy.
- Investigations typically complete within 10 business days.

## We will never
- Ask for your full password or one-time passcode.
- Ask you to move money to a "safe account".

High-risk situations (fraud, scams, identity theft, threats) are always escalated to a trained human specialist.
