# Cards: Debit & Credit

## Lost or stolen card
1. Open the mobile app and go to Card Controls.
2. Tap "Freeze card" to immediately block new transactions.
3. If the card was stolen or you see unknown transactions, tap "Report & replace" or call the 24/7 number on our website.
Freezing a card is reversible; reporting it stolen permanently cancels it and orders a new one.

## Card delivery
Replacement cards arrive within 5–7 business days by standard post. Express delivery (1–2 days) may be available for a fee.

## Contactless & limits
The default contactless limit is set by the card network and shown in the app. Per-transaction and daily limits can be viewed in Card Controls. Changing a limit requires identity verification and is handled by the secure app flow — not by chat support.

## Activating a new card
New cards are activated in the app or at any ATM using your PIN. Support agents cannot activate cards on your behalf.
